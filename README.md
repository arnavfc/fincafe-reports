# FinCafe Pre-Market Reports

Public feed for the daily **FinCafe – Stock Market Institute** Pre-Market Report, shown on
[www.fincaferesearch.com](https://www.fincaferesearch.com/#premarket).

- `reports.json`: list of published reports, newest first (date, headline, 5 points, PDF and cover paths).
- `reports/YYYY/MM/`: the approved PDF and its cover image for each day.

Only reports approved by FinCafe are published here, by `publish_site.py`.
Information and education only. Not investment advice.

Get the report free every trading day at 8 AM on the FinCafe WhatsApp channel:
https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029VbAl4smD8SDylX1pdb3U
